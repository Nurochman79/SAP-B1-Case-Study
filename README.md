## Cakupan Modul & Konfigurasi:
1. **Master Data & Chart of Accounts (CoA):** G/L Account Determination, Business Partner (Vendor & Customer), Item Master Data.
2. **Procurement Cycle (P2P):** Purchase Order -> Goods Receipt PO -> A/P Invoice -> Outgoing Payment.
3. **Sales Cycle (O2C):** Sales Quotation -> Sales Order -> Delivery -> A/R Invoice -> Incoming Payment.
4. **Inventory & Accounting:** Transfer Stok antar gudang, Jurnal Umum Manual.
5. **Production & MRP:** Pembuatan Bill of Materials (BOM), Issue/Receipt for Production, & Production Order Execution.
